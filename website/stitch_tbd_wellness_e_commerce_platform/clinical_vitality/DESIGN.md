## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes high-performance nutriceutical precision with editorial luxury. Designed for high-performing individuals, biohackers, and wellness connoisseurs, it communicates absolute clinical rigor, efficacy, and pristine biomedical purity.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern** precision with **Minimalist** clinical elegance:
- **Atmospheric Purity:** Expansive, breathing white and tinted ice-blue canvases evoke sterile laboratory precision and pure hydration.
- **Architectural Structure:** Inspired by rigorous clinical layouts and direct-to-consumer biohacking interfaces, components utilize hairline structural divisions, modular metric displays, and clinical data badges.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Deep oceanic blues and luminous electric aqua accents mirror cellular energy, vitality, and advanced formulation delivery.

## Colors

The color palette reflects clinical integrity and cellular vitality:

- **Primary (`#075CA8` - Royal Blue):** The authoritative anchor representing pharmaceutical grade validation, used for primary action items, key headers, and featured product frames.
- **Secondary (`#008FD3` - Electric Aqua):** Represents active hydration and bioavailability; applied to secondary callouts, interactive states, and active ingredient badges.
- **Tertiary (`#16B5E6` - Vibrant Cyan):** An energetic accent for micro-interactions, progress bars, and vital statistic highlights.
- **Neutral (`#061B3A` - Deep Navy):** Replaces harsh standard blacks with an ultra-deep maritime slate for typography, navigation structures, and high-contrast editorial blocks.
- **Surfaces & Borders:** `#FFFFFF` serves as the primary canvas, accompanied by `#F5F7FA` (Soft Gray) and `#E9F6FD` (Ice Blue) for container backgrounds. Subtle hairline delineations use `#D8E2EA` (Refined Silver).

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts structural innovation with ultra-legible utilitarian beauty:

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Engineered geometry with tight negative tracking gives product headlines, scientific claims, and metrics an assertive, avant-garde clinical presence.
- **Body & UI Elements (Inter):** High x-height, neutral letterforms provide fatigue-free readability for complex botanical ingredient lists, dosage schedules, clinical trials, and checkout funnels.
- **Micro-labels & Scientific Tags:** Set in uppercase Space Grotesk (`label-caps`) with expanded tracking (`0.12em`) to mirror pharmaceutical batch stamps and nutraceutical classifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is constructed on a strict 8px harmonic grid, optimizing information hierarchy between editorial lifestyle photography and clinical specification panels.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop screens utilize a 12-column fluid grid system with a maximum canvas width of 1320px. Tablet adapts to an 8-column layout, and mobile converges to a 4-column stack.
- **Section Margins:** Desktop features generous `3rem` (48px) margins, scaling to `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports to maximize clinical visual real estate.
- **Content Rhythms:** Component interior paddings adhere to `space-md` (16px) for compact cards and `space-xl` (40px) for prominent subscription modules. Scientific comparison tables and ingredient dossiers rely on compact vertical spacing (`space-sm`) to encourage direct comparison.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from heavy, murky drop shadows, opting for pristine surface-layering and tactile clinical elevation:

- **Layered Tonal Surfaces:** Visual hierarchy is formed by layering `#FFFFFF` cards over subtle `#F5F7FA` or `#E9F6FD` canvases.
- **Hairline Boundaries:** Crisp, 1px borders using `#D8E2EA` define modules, reminiscent of precision diagnostic apparatus.
- **Ambient Hydro-Glow Shadows:** Elevated components (hovered cards, purchase trays, modal sheets) project an ethereal blue-tinted diffusion: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(7, 92, 168, 0.08)`.
- **Floating Sticky Trays:** E-commerce quick-add bars utilize backdrop-filtered glass with subtle diffusion: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); border-top: 1px solid rgba(216, 226, 234, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **3 (Pill-shaped)** for user interaction touchpoints, balanced against soft architectural contours for containment:

- **Buttons & Pills:** All primary action drivers, dosage selectors, and category pills utilize full 9999px pill radii (`rounded-full`), reflecting the ergonomic curves of premium compressed tablets and softgels.
- **Containers & Product Cards:** Module corners utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) to soften clinical precision into an approachable luxury wellness experience.
- **Inner Micro-Elements:** Progress meters, verification badges, and nutrition tag chips maintain fully circular or soft pill finishes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill CTA):** Solid `#075CA8` background, `#FFFFFF` Space Grotesk text, full pill radius (`9999px`), with generous horizontal padding (32px) and 52px height. Hover state shifts smoothly to `#082B58` with a micro-lift (`translateY(-1px)`) and ambient hydro glow.
- **Secondary / Scientific:** Bordered pill with 1.5px `#075CA8` stroke, transparent or white background, `#075CA8` typography. Hover transforms background to `#E9F6FD`.
- **Ghost / Text:** Deep navy typography with an animated electric aqua hairline underline expanding on interaction.

### Chips & Badges
- **Nutraceutical Verification Chip:** Pill silhouette featuring a `#E9F6FD` background, `#075CA8` label, and an optional 6px circular `#16B5E6` pulse indicator for lab-certified claims.
- **Ingredient Dosing Tag:** Micro-chip with `#F5F7FA` fill and `#061B3A` text for specifying milligrams (e.g., "500mg Ashwagandha").

### Cards & Product Modules
- **Subscription / Purchase Selector Card:** Encased in 1px `#D8E2EA` border with a 24px inner padding. Active selection activates a dual border with a 1.5px `#075CA8` ring and subtle `#E9F6FD` tinted background.
- **Clinical Study Card:** High-contrast editorial card pairing Space Grotesk statistics (e.g., "+47% Cellular Energy") against clean Inter trial explanations.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Crisp 48px height with 1px `#D8E2EA` border, `#FFFFFF` interior, and `rounded-lg` (16px) contour. Focus state triggers a 1.5px `#008FD3` ring with zero outline offset.
- **Radio & Checkboxes:** Circular radio buttons featuring an animated inward-expanding `#075CA8` core against a `#E9F6FD` inactive track.

### Lists & Ingredient Dossiers
- **Supplement Facts Accordion:** Flush edge-to-edge listing with hairline `#D8E2EA` separators, bold Space Grotesk nutrient titles, and tab-aligned botanical dosage breakdowns.
